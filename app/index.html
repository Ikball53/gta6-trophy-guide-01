    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, built from the official descriptions: story chapters and named missions first, collectibles and counts in free roam, 100% completion last. Tick trophies off here or in the checklist — progress is shared and saved in your browser.

    Missable trophies — read before you play

    Eight trophies are tied to one-time story moments, choices or set-pieces in their official descriptions. Keep a manual save before each chapter's late missions and before the final mission; if one of these moments passes, reload that save and replay the stretch. Everything else can be cleaned up in free roam after the story.

    Save, export & settings

    Progress is saved automatically in this browser (localStorage) the moment you tick a trophy or move a counter. Export it to a file to back it up or move it to another browser, and import that file here to restore it. Nothing leaves your device: this guide makes no network requests.

    Where the facts come from

    Official names, descriptions, grades and tracked counts follow the PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every entry. Each trophy's guide names its own sources beside it in the checklist. Guides are original text written for this entry — sources are cited, never copied — and no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot appears anywhere in this guide.
